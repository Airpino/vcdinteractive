# vcdinteractive 0.1.0

* First release.
* `imosaic()`: interactive mosaic, association, sieve and doubledecker plots,
  with variable reordering, inclusion and removal, split directions, six
  residual types, comparison of log-linear models, fitted `glm`/`gnm` models
  as input, and the list of significant cells.
* `mosaic_cells()`, `significant_cells()`: the cell statistics in R.
* `ipairs()`, `ifourfold()`, `irootogram()`, `ispine()`, `icdplot()`,
  `imcaplot()`, `iassoc_graph()`: interactive counterparts of the other
  displays of 'vcd' and 'vcdExtra'.
* Seven vignettes.
